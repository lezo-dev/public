## 2. Roles of LEZO and the Customer

LEZO acts as data controller for its own processing operations, including website management, accounts, authentication, security, support, technical administration, billing, service improvement, legal obligations and defense of its rights.

When Questovery is used by a professional customer to create, administer or operate a quest, that customer may act as data controller for data related to its participants, players, teams, content, routes, media, participation conditions, commercial offers and relationships with its own users. In this case, LEZO processes certain data on behalf of the customer, as processor.

The customer remains responsible for informing its own users where processing falls within its purposes, content, participation rules or relationship with them.

## 3. Data We Process

Depending on your use, we may process the following categories of data:

- account and identification data: email address, identifier, first name, last name, pseudonym, role, organization, team, account status;
- authentication and security data: securely stored passwords, login links or codes, connection logs, security events;
- content data: quests, steps, texts, questions, answers, hints, scenarios, media, images, sounds, videos, files, team and player names;
- participation data: joined quest, team, progress, answers, score, ranking, history, feedback, game status, timestamps;
- location data, where the feature is enabled and permission is granted: GPS position, geolocated validation events, movement trace during an active quest;
- notification data: push token, preferences and sending status where notifications are enabled;
- payment and billing data: plan, prepaid credits, order references, transaction identifiers, payment status, invoices and information necessary to manage payment;
- technical data: IP address, device model, operating system, browser, app version, technical logs, errors, diagnostic and performance data;
- support data: requests, exchanges, attachments, information shared to resolve a question or incident.

## 5. Geolocation

Location is used only if the feature is enabled in the relevant quest and you grant the necessary permissions on your device.

Location may be used to unlock nearby steps, validate geolocated objectives, record a trace during an active game, display a route or produce results linked to the quest.

Where background location permission is granted, trace recording may continue while the application is not displayed, only during an active quest and for the needs of the relevant feature.

You can withdraw location permissions at any time from your device settings. Some quest features may then no longer work properly.
